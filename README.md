# Awesome-Digital-Library-Platform

# Top Digital Library Platform Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Ebook Lending, Digital Content Platforms, Academic eBook Access, PressReader-Style Delivery & Library Digital Collections*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Library Platforms**. These systems deliver ebooks, digital magazines, and scholarly content to library patrons and students—often with DRM, concurrent-user models, and discovery integration.

**Examples** include OverDrive, ProQuest Ebook Central, EBSCO eBooks, Perlego, BibliU, Kortext, VitalSource, Wheelers ePlatform, Odilo, and PressReader (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Licensed ebook marketplaces are commercial. Open strength is in **ILS/LSP** (Koha, FOLIO, Evergreen), **repositories** (DSpace, Invenio), and **self-hosted reading** stacks (Calibre-Web, Kavita). This section lists every significant relevant project found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[OverDrive, Odilo, Wheelers ePlatform](https://www.overdrive.com/)**  
  Public and school library digital lending platforms for ebooks, audiobooks, and magazines.

- **[ProQuest Ebook Central, EBSCO eBooks](https://about.proquest.com/)**  
  Academic ebook aggregators integrated with library discovery and course workflows.

- **[VitalSource, Kortext, BibliU, Perlego](https://www.vitalsource.com/)**  
  Student-focused digital textbook and inclusive-access platforms for higher education.

- **[PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/)**  
  Digital newspapers and magazines platform widely offered through libraries and hospitality.

- **[Other commercial digital library platforms](https://www.overdrive.com/)**  
  Additional metered-access and publisher-direct ebook services.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Koha](https://gitlab.com/koha-community/koha)**  
  Leading open-source integrated library system (ILS)—catalog, circulation, and digital-content integration points for public and academic libraries.

- **[FOLIO](https://github.com/folio-org)**  
  Open-source Library Services Platform—modular LSP for academic libraries with strong electronic resource management (ERM) focus.

- **[Evergreen](https://github.com/evergreen-library-system/Evergreen)**  
  Open ILS designed for consortia and large public-library networks with shared catalogs.

- **[DSpace](https://github.com/DSpace/DSpace)**  
  Leading open institutional repository—digital collections, theses, and open-access content management.

- **[Invenio / Zenodo software](https://github.com/inveniosoftware)**  
  Open repository framework powering large-scale research and digital library repositories.

- **[Calibre-Web / Kavita](https://github.com/janeczku/calibre-web)**  
  Open self-hosted ebook servers—personal or small-org digital libraries with browser reading (not a substitute for licensed publisher catalogs).

- **[Greenstone](https://www.greenstone.org/)**  
  Open-source digital library software for building and distributing collections, especially in education and NGOs.

- **[VuFind / Blacklight discovery](https://github.com/vufind-org/vufind)**  
  Open discovery layers that unify physical and digital library resources for patrons.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Full ILS**: Koha for most libraries; Evergreen for consortia; FOLIO for academic LSP needs.
- **Repositories**: DSpace or Invenio for institutional digital collections.
- **Self-hosted reading**: Calibre-Web/Kavita for owned/open content only.
- **Composable stacks**: Koha/FOLIO + discovery (VuFind) + repository (DSpace) + licensed content APIs from commercial vendors.
- Commercial digital lending still required for mainstream publisher ebook catalogs and simultaneous-use models.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Koha** or **FOLIO** for library operations; **DSpace** for open collections; **Calibre-Web** only for rights-cleared content.  
Patron ebook borrowing from major publishers depends on commercial platforms (OverDrive, ProQuest, EBSCO, VitalSource, etc.).  
Libraries typically run open ILS/repositories and subscribe to commercial digital content platforms. Fully open digital *lending of commercial ebooks* is not realistic due to publisher licensing; open stacks excel at catalog, discovery, and open-access content.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Respect copyright and publisher licenses. Self-hosted readers must only serve content you have rights to distribute. Library lending models are contractual—not purely technical.
- Open-source ILS and repositories offer sovereignty and cost control but require skilled systems librarians. Commercial digital content platforms provide licensed catalogs and vendor support. Neither replaces collection development policy and patron privacy safeguards.
